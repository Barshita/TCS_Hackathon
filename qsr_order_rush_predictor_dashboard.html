Model Engineering Assumptions & Logic

1. Censored Demand Correction (+10% Stockout Adjustment)

In QSR logs, when Unsold_Qty == 0 and Sold_Qty == Prepared_Qty, customer demand was likely capped by kitchen stockouts. We apply a +10% uncensoring factor to those historical slots so popular items are not systematically under-predicted.

2. Strict Perishability Window (Zero Carryover)

Prepared hot food items cannot be carried over across dayparts (e.g., Lunch to Evening). Any unit prepared above actual period demand is recorded as 100% wastage at the end of that time period.

3. Context-Weighted Forecasting & Newsvendor Buffer

Demand is predicted via Baseline(Item, DayType, Period) × Event_Lift × Promo_Lift. Recommended prep quantity uses a modified Newsvendor safety buffer: high-wastage items (>15% historical unsold rate) have their safety buffer dampened to protect margins, while fast-moving low-waste items receive an upward buffer to prevent wait times.

4. Pre-Period Kitchen Lead Time

Forecasts are surfaced prior to period start based on each item's prep_time_mins (ranging from 4 mins for beverages/fries to 14 mins for grilled wraps/burgers).
